The property

An attractive detached family house set back from one of Hadley Wood's quiet tree-lined avenues behind electric gates. The accommodation is arranged over two floors and has been carefully modernised by the current owners.

The ground floor centres on a large kitchen/family room across the back of the house, with patio doors opening onto a stone terrace and a level lawn. There are separate sitting and dining rooms, a study, a cloakroom and a utility room with a door to the integral garage. Upstairs, the principal bedroom has its own dressing room and en-suite, and there are four further bedrooms and a family bathroom.
